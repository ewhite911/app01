# Working rules for this repo

## 1. Don't change things on your own

Do what was asked. Nothing more.

If you notice something else that should change — wording, layout, colours, a
feature, a structure — **say what you found and wait**. Do not fix it in the
same breath as the thing you were asked to do.

This has gone wrong before. Asked to show store screenshots, the copy was
rewritten and the photographs swapped out. Asked to look at the routine screen,
the whole screen was redesigned. None of it was requested.

The line: a bug that blocks the thing you were asked to do is yours to fix.
Anything you merely think would be better is a sentence to the user, not a
commit.

## 2. Keep what you show simple

When reporting a result, a value or a decision:

- Short sentences. Plain words.
- Say the answer first. Reasoning after, and only if it changes what to do.
- No long lists of everything you considered.
- No walls of explanation about your own process.

The user is busy and reads on a phone. Two or three sentences is usually right.
