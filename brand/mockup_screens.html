How this app is run

No ads

Not now, not later. Nothing in this app is sold to anyone else.

No investors

Nobody is waiting on this app to grow. It only has to pay for itself.

The store keeps 15%

Of every $3.99, Google or Apple takes 15% before it reaches the maker.

One person

One person writes the code, picks the verses and answers support mail.
What is left covers the store accounts, the developer fees and the hours that go into the next update.

Four things that will not change

1
What is free today stays free. The free list will not be made smaller later.
2
No ads will be added to this app.
3
Your prayers are not sold, shared or uploaded. They stay on this phone.
4
Cancelling takes three taps, and you will never be asked why.
