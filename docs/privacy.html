Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026 · applies to Selah Daily version 1.0

Selah Daily has no user accounts. Your verses, your prayer requests and your routine history are written to a database file on your phone and stay there. There is exactly one way they leave it, and you have to start it yourself: moving your list to a new phone. Even then, what crosses is locked with a code that never leaves your phone, and it is deleted within 24 hours.

Who is responsible

Selah Daily is made and operated by an individual developer in the Republic of Korea. For any question about this policy or your data, write to SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE. Messages are read by the developer, not by a team.

None of this is uploaded on its own, and none of it is shared. There is no sign-up, no login, no email collection and no advertising identifier. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, so no usage data is sent anywhere. The single exception is the transfer described below, which only ever runs when you ask for it.

Moving to a new phone

Settings → Move to a new phone is the one feature that sends anything off your device. It is worth being exact about what happens, because the rest of this policy depends on it staying narrow.

  • Your old phone makes a ten-character code and uses it to derive an AES-256-GCM key. Your prayers and routine history are encrypted on the phone with that key.
  • Only the encrypted result and a one-way hash of the code are sent to our transfer service, which runs on Supabase. The code itself and the key are never sent. Nobody operating the service — including us — can read what is stored there.
  • Your new phone sends the same one-way hash, receives the encrypted block, and decrypts it locally with the code you typed in.
  • The stored block is deleted the moment a phone claims it, and in any case 24 hours after it was created. It is never used for anything else, and no record of the transfer is kept.

If you would rather nothing touched a server at all, Settings → Export prayers and Import from a file do the same job with a file you move yourself.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications. Used only to show reminders that your own phone schedules: the daily reminder you set, and a reminder two days before a free trial ends. No push notifications are sent from any server, and no push token is collected.
  • Calendar (optional). If you choose "Add to calendar" when a trial starts, the app writes one event to the calendar you select and can remove that event when you cancel. The app does not read, upload or analyse your other calendar entries. Declining this permission changes nothing else in the app.

Purchases

Membership is billed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us. When you start a trial or subscribe, the store and our purchase provider, RevenueCat, process the purchase. To check whether a membership is active, the app sends RevenueCat an anonymous identifier generated on your device, plus the purchase receipt and basic device and store information. Your name, your email address and your prayer requests are never sent — they are not available to that layer of the app at all. We never receive your card details; only the store handles payment.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export. Settings → Export prayers writes a JSON file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes after that is your choice.
  • Import. Settings → Import from a file reads a JSON export back in. It adds to what is on the phone; it never replaces it.
  • Delete. Uninstalling the app deletes the database with it. Individual requests can be deleted in the app by holding an item and choosing Delete. Nothing about you is stored on our side once a transfer expires or is claimed, so there is nothing for us to delete for you and no deletion request to file. If you started a transfer and changed your mind, simply do not use the code: it expires within 24 hours.

Children

Selah Daily is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information, and it has no account, no chat, no user-generated content shared with anyone, and no ads.

Scripture and content

Bible text is the Berean Standard Bible, which is in the public domain. The app is non-denominational and carries no political content. It is not counselling, medical advice or pastoral care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what leaves your phone will also be stated in the app's release notes. We will not start collecting personal data in a later version without saying so plainly.
